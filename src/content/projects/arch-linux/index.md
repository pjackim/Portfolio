The goal was to become comfortable using Linux. I love clean interfaces, and I figured that customizing everything would eventually lead to understanding Linux. It worked.

I started with zero experience and learned package installation, terminal navigation, Bash scripting, file-system structure, permissions and general commands, plus Vim, maintainability, GitHub and iterative UX design. My configurations are on [GitHub](https://github.com/pjackim/dots).

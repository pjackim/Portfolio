Paradox was a video-game hacking group that offered subscriptions across nearly every gaming platform. I joined when I was ten, and it was a pillar of my technical development.

My role revolved around design: advertisements, graphical user interfaces, branding elements and promotional videos. My first banner was for its predecessor, zExileModz, and I created that team's logo too; once Paradox was established, we reworked all the branding to reflect the new name. At the time I didn't know how to program, so my contributions to the software were graphical and design elements. Much of my early work there has been lost to time.
